# Chat history

**[RULINGS_AND_INSTRUCTIONS.md](RULINGS_AND_INSTRUCTIONS.md)** — every human instruction given to the
working sessions that built this estate, chronological and verbatim (pseudonymized). This is the
decision record: when a rule is disputed, it settles what was actually asked for, and when.

The raw session transcripts that used to sit in `sessions/` have been **removed from the whole
history**, not just from the current tree. They were bulk person data by nature — every prospect
ever discussed, in context, with company and title — and pseudonymizing names inside them still left
them re-identifiable. They remain on the original operator's machine only.
