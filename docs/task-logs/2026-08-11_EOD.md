## Two regressions I introduced today

1. **Candidate off-by-one.** `completeExpression = $pageCount >= (cands.length - 1)`; n8n evaluates
   it with `$pageCount` already incremented, so it stopped one candidate short. With four duplicate
   persons it queried three and skipped the one holding the lead. **Caught by
   `__leads_page_saturated`** (3 read < 4 candidates) — the coverage flag worked. My statics could
   not have caught it: they execute the node body against mocked pages, and the defect lived in the
   pagination expression, which exists only at runtime (§9c.2c).
2. **Duplicate forward — and this one FIRED, it was not a prediction.** The no-lead guard correctly
   kept a half-processed reply replayable, but a replay re-runs the whole branch including the
   forward. Exec **15252** sent a second forward for the fixture at 13:45:56Z. Fixed with a separate
   `F|<dedupe_key>` claimed on send success and gating the send; 10/10 statics including the
   end-to-end replay case (forward once → replay → CRM retried, **zero second forward**). All 20
   already-forwarded events seeded so no armed replay can re-fire; 8 of those are inside the 48h
   replay window, the other 12 are older than WF-2R can reach.
